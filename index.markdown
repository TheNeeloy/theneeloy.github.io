## Nice to meet you!
I'm a 5th year PhD candidate studying electrical and computer engineering at the [University of Illinois Urbana Champaign](https://illinois.edu/). I am currently conducting research under [Professor Katie Driggs-Campbell](https://krdc.web.illinois.edu/) in the [Human-Centered Autonomy Lab](https://thehcalab.web.illinois.edu/). My primary research revolves around developing reliable technologies to enable safer interactions between humans and automation. To tackle this challenge, I am developing **anomaly aware teams**: human-robot teams with modules to proactively identify poor decisions, detect failures in real-time, and react to said failures with human guidance. Prior to this, I received my B.S. and M.S. in electrical and computer engineering from the University of Illinois in 2021 and 2023, respectively.
